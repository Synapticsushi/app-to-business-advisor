# App to Business Advisor

> You built the app. Now turn it into a business.

AI helped you build something. This free, open-source skill helps first-time builders figure out what comes next: what to charge, how to find a first customer, how to run a paid pilot, and how to make a clear offer without startup jargon or invented benchmarks.

It is a framework for better decisions, not a benchmark database or a substitute for legal, tax, financial, security, or regulatory advice.

## What it does

- Gives a clear recommendation with assumptions and trade-offs.
- Bases pricing on buyer value, alternatives, and fully loaded cost to serve.
- Requires current sources for market facts and customer-facing claims.
- Turns uncertainty into a small validation experiment instead of invented certainty.
- Structures pilots around scope, success measures, end dates, and conversion decisions.

## Install

Download or clone this repository, then install the skill through your Codex or compatible Agent Skills workflow.

## Example prompts

- “Use the SaaS Business Advisor to package a field-service scheduling app for independent contractors.”
- “Help me structure a paid design-partner pilot for a compliance workflow tool.”
- “Give me a defensible pricing experiment for a logistics dashboard with two early customers.”
- “Draft a discovery plan before I quote an annual contract for a healthcare operations product.”

## Boundaries

The skill does not claim market statistics without current sources. Have qualified professionals review legal terms, taxes, IP ownership, privacy, security, and regulatory obligations.

## Contributing

Contributions should improve reusable process, sourcing discipline, or examples. Do not add customer secrets, uncited benchmarks, or jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions presented as universal rules.
